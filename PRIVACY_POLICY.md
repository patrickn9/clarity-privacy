# Privacy Policy for Clarity

**Last Updated: February 8, 2026**

## Introduction

Clarity ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our brain health tracking application.

## Information We Collect

### Health Data
Clarity accesses the following types of health data from Apple Health (HealthKit) with your explicit permission:

- **Heart Rate Variability (HRV)**: Used to assess autonomic nervous system health
- **Sleep Data**: Total sleep hours and sleep stages for cognitive recovery analysis
- **Physical Activity**: Step count and active energy for activity assessment
- **Heart Rate**: Resting heart rate measurements for cardiovascular health
- **Stress Levels**: Derived stress indicators for mental wellness tracking

### Data Storage
- **All health data remains on your device only**
- Data is stored locally using Apple's SwiftData framework
- No health data is ever transmitted to external servers
- No cloud backup of health data occurs

### Non-Health Data
- **App usage data**: Which features you interact with (stored locally only)
- **Settings preferences**: Your customization choices (stored locally only)

## How We Use Your Information

We use your health data exclusively to:
1. Calculate your daily Brain Health Score (0-100)
2. Generate personalized health insights and recommendations
3. Display trends and visualizations of your health metrics over time
4. Provide actionable feedback to support cognitive wellness

**All processing happens on your device using Apple's on-device frameworks.**

## Data Sharing

**We do not share, sell, or transmit your health data to anyone.**

- No third-party services have access to your health data
- No advertising networks receive your information
- No analytics companies track your health metrics
- Your data never leaves your iPhone

## Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

- **Access**: View all your health data within the app
- **Deletion**: Delete your data at any time via Settings → Delete All Data
- **Revoke Access**: Remove HealthKit permissions in iOS Settings → Privacy → Health → Clarity
- **Export**: Export your data via Settings → Export Data

## Data Retention

- Health data is retained on your device until you choose to delete it
- If you delete the app, all locally stored data is permanently removed
- No data backups exist on external servers

## Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:
- All data is protected by iOS system-level encryption
- Data is stored in Apple's secure SwiftData containers
- HealthKit access follows Apple's strict security protocols
- No internet connection is required for core functionality

## Children's Privacy

Clarity is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect health information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be reflected in the app and on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
